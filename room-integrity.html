Evidence gate · before drawing a public conclusion
  1. Verify the source record and preserve the original file.
  2. Use a primary record to document or refute a specific claim.
  3. Link an official or agency only with authoritative, time-bounded identifiers.
  4. Confirm a repeated pattern with independent evidence and a valid denominator.
  5. Require two-person and appropriate legal/policy review before any public finding.
Source-record counts may reflect population, charging volume, reporting, recordkeeping, group cases, advocacy, and review capacity. They do not establish intent, causation, or institutional responsibility. Review data is kept out of the story generator and cloud savepoints.
