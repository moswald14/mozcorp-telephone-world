Eat. Evolve. Defeat the frog.

Choose a starting bug in Eat & Evolve. Move with WASD, arrows, or tap to walk. The prey buttons chase a bug for you. Get close and press Eat / Space to catch it, then press again in the green timing window to learn its power.

A rushed bite eats the bug but gives no skill and costs health. Three missed passes let it escape. Eat the same species cleanly to raise its skill to rank III. Use 1–4 or the skill buttons. Slower bite timing is available.

After three clean bites and three learned skills, enter the frog pond. Move off the pink warning line, then bite or use powers while the frog rests. Retry keeps learned skills. P pauses.
