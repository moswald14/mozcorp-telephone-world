What a matching checksum means

SHA-256 matches the saved fields. It does not authenticate an author, prove a sensation or weather report, certify the device clock, or prove a draw was fair.

EW-C14N-1: fixed versioned schemas, JSON keys sorted recursively in UTF-16 order, no whitespace, UTF-8 bytes. Strings are preserved during verification. New text is normalized to NFC before stamping. Each receipt payload and its separate linked THRUM payload have their own checksum.
